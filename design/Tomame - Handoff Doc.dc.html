Tomame

UI redesign — handoff

Design direction, files, and build notes for the Tomame app and marketing site. 10 September 2026.

Scope

App: Home, Landed price, Bag & pay, Journeys, Journey detail, Account, Mobile. Marketing: Landing, Where we buy, Fees, About.

Business rules baked in

Ship from the USA only today — UK and China shown as “coming soon”. No personal US address. Journey stage is Purchased, not Bought. GH₵ first, $ second on every price.

  • Type: Bricolage Grotesque 700/800 for headings (tight tracking −0.02 to −0.035em); Instrument Sans 400–700 for everything else. Prices use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.
  • Neutrals: #6E625B secondary text, #8A7F79 tertiary, #F0E8E3 borders, #F5EEE9 hairlines. Amber #C97A0A for “in progress / soon”.
  • Shape: cards 20–26px radius, 1px #F0E8E3 border, no shadows except floating hero cards. Pills 999px. Buttons 44–52px tall, 12–14px radius; primary = brand gradient.
  • Icons: Phosphor (duotone for feature icons, fill for status, bold for arrows, regular in-line). Not Lucide.
  • Black: used for text only — no dark surfaces, no dark header.

3. Motion

All animation is CSS keyframes, ease cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), and must respect prefers-reduced-motion.

  • tmUp — 14px rise + fade, 0.5–0.7s, staggered 60–80ms per card on page load.
  • Receipt print-in — landed-price rows appear one by one (150ms apart), total pops (tmPop) after the last row.
  • tmFill — bars scale from left (worked example on Fees, freight box on Home).
  • tmFloat — hero cards drift ±8px over 6–7s.
  • tmPlane / tmArc — plane glides along the journey track (10s loop) and along the US→Accra arc (6s loop, offset-path).
  • tmWords — hero store name cycles Amazon → eBay → Walmart → Best Buy every ~2.2s.

6. Build notes

  • Nav: app nav (TmNavLight) is white with a search/paste bar, Bag count, Price watch, Bell, avatar. Tabs: Home · Shop · Price watch · Journeys · Account. Replaces the marketing links in dashboard-navbar / main/navbar.
  • Pricing copy: item · US sales tax · Tomame fee 5% · freight (GH₵) · rate (locked 24h). Total labelled “At your door”. Keep the existing pricing helpers in src/config/pricing.ts; only the labels change.
  • Journey stages map to existing OrderStatus: pending→Awaiting payment, paid→Paid, processing→Being purchased, in_transit→In the air, delivered/completed→Delivered.
  • Bag is new state (multi-item, grouped by consolidation box). Freight box % = total weight / box cap; surface “save GH₵X with one more item”.
  • Regions: only US is purchasable. UK/CN render disabled with a waitlist CTA — don't build routing for them yet.
  • Photos: every marketing image is a labelled drop-zone; captions describe the shot. Generation prompts are in the chat thread. Product images use striped placeholders until real data is wired.
  • Copy fixtures: names, prices and dates in the mocks are sample data — swap for live.
